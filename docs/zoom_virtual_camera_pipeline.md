## 🌟 Pipeline Overview

When you receive a Zoom meeting link (e.g., from a professor), follow these steps to join the meeting with your **Sign Avatar** acting as your camera feed!

### 🛠️ Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have the following ready:

1. **🎭 Sign Avatar Running**: Your Sign Avatar application must be running and actively broadcasting its virtual camera feed.

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1️⃣ Prepare the Zoom Link 🔗
*   **Get the Link**: Grab the Zoom meeting link from your professor or organizer.
*   **Force Browser Join 🌐**: *This is crucial!* When you click the link, Zoom naturally tries to open the desktop app. To stay in the browser:
    1. ❌ Cancel the pop-up prompt asking to "Open Zoom Meetings".
    2. 🔄 Click the "Launch Meeting" button on the webpage again.
    3. ❌ Cancel the pop-up prompt a second time.
    4. 👇 A new link will appear at the bottom saying **"Join from Your Browser"**. Click it!

### 2️⃣ Join the Zoom Meeting 🤝
*   **Enter Name**: Before you enter the room, Zoom will ask for your name. Type exactly:  
    <br>
    <div align="center">
      <h2>👤 <code>Sign Avatar</code></h2>
    </div>
*   **Sign In (If needed)**: If the meeting requires authentication, sign in, but double-check that your display name remains "Sign Avatar".

### 3️⃣ Configure Camera Settings in Zoom ⚙️
*   **Allow Permissions**: Your browser will ask for permission to use your mic and camera. Click **"Allow"** ✅.
*   **Select Camera**: Look for the "Start/Stop Video" button at the bottom of the Zoom screen. Click the small up-arrow (`^`) next to it.
*   **Choose Virtual Camera**: From the list, select the Sign Avatar virtual camera.
*   **Action! 🎬**: Turn on your video. The Zoom meeting will now broadcast your stunning Sign Avatar feed to everyone!

> ⬇️ *streams to*
> **👨‍🏫 Zoom Meeting Room**
